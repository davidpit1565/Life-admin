# Support

Life Admin is a bills, renewals, and reminders app for iOS. If something isn't working, or you
have a question or a feature suggestion, email:

**dp@solfaygroup.com**

Please include your iOS version and, if you're reporting a bug, a short description of what you
were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**Does Life Admin require an account?**
No. There's no login and no account of any kind.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device only. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html) for the full breakdown of
what's stored and what, if anything, ever leaves your device.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes — Settings → Export gives you a full backup as a JSON file you can save or share anywhere you
choose.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app deletes everything stored on-device. There's no server-side copy to separately
request deletion of.
