## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Life Admin keeps track of the recurring stuff that's easy to forget — bills, insurance renewals,
subscriptions, warranties, appointments, and any other due date you're responsible for.

ADD THINGS THE WAY YOU THINK
Type or dictate what you need to track — "Car insurance renews March 3rd, $890" — and Life Admin
figures out the category, amount, and due date. No forms to fill out first.

NEVER MISS A RENEWAL
Get reminded before something's due, with flexible lead times (the same day, a day before, a week
before, a month before, or more). Reminders can sync to your iOS Reminders and Calendar if you
want them there too.

BUILT FOR EACH KIND OF ITEM
A passport shows passport fields. A credit card shows card fields. A bill shows amount and due
date. Add your own custom fields to any item, and lock sensitive ones behind Face ID / Touch ID.

SEE WHAT'S OVERLAPPING
The Insights tab can flag categories where you have more than one active recurring item — a
useful nudge if you've lost track of what you're actually still paying for.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Life Admin has no accounts and no login. Everything you enter lives on your device. Nothing is
sold or shared for marketing, ever. The optional AI feature (which you can turn off entirely) only
ever sends the text you're adding — never your photos, your contacts, or your calendar — to
extract a title, amount, and date. Export a full backup any time you want a copy.

PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN
- No account creation, no login
- No analytics or advertising SDKs
- No tracking of any kind
- Face ID / Touch ID lock for sensitive items
- Full data export whenever you want it

Life Admin is a reminder and organization tool, not financial, legal, insurance, or immigration
advice.
```
